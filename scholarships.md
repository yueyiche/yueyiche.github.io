## Overview

This is a curated list of scholarships, grants, fellowships, and research opportunities that I have compiled since my undergraduate years.

- Many opportunities are **open to international students in the U.S.**
- Especially relevant for:
  - Earth science
  - Cryosphere science
  - Paleoclimate
  - Geology
  - Field-based research

Some opportunities are tied to local chapters, but similar programs often exist elsewhere.

## How to Use This Page

1. Browse the **timeline below (organized by deadline)**  
2. Use **Ctrl/Cmd + F** to quickly locate opportunities  
3. Scroll down for **detailed application information**

If you notice outdated links or missing opportunities, feel free to reach out via the contact page.
